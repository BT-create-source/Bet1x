How to play Astronaut

  1. Place a bet during the countdown. You can play two bets per round, one on each panel.
  2. The astronaut launches and the multiplier climbs from 1.00x.
  3. Press CASH OUT before the astronaut is lost in space to win your bet × the multiplier.
  4. If the astronaut is lost before you cash out, that bet is lost.
  5. Bet during a flight to queue it for the next round. A bet can be cancelled until the countdown ends.

Auto

  • Auto bet places the same bet at the start of every round until you switch it off.
  • Auto cash out cashes out automatically the moment your multiplier is reached, even if you close the page.

Provably fair

Every round's crash point is fixed before the countdown, from a secret seed whose SHA-256 hash is shown during the countdown. The seed is revealed after the crash, and the crash point is floor(100 × RTP ÷ (1 − u)) ÷ 100, where u is the first 13 hex digits of HMAC-SHA256(seed, "astronaut:" + round) ÷ 16¹³.
