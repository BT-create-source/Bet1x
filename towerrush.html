How to play

  1. Set your bet and press BUILD. The crane drops its swinging block onto your tower.
  2. Every floor that lands shows a multiplier. Floors multiply together — some raise your total, some (below x1) lower it.
  3. Press CASHOUT at any time to take your bet × the running total.
  4. If a floor shows x0 the tower falls and the bet is lost.

Shortcuts: Space build · C cash out

Floor multipliers

Provably fair

Every floor is decided when you place the bet, from a secret server seed whose SHA-256 is shown before the first drop. The seed is revealed when the tower ends: floor n uses HMAC-SHA256(seed, "floor:n") (first 13 hex digits ÷ 16¹³) — below the collapse chance is x0, otherwise it picks a multiplier by the weights above.
