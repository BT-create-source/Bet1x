How to play Chicken Road

  1. Choose your bet and a difficulty, then press PLAY. The hen steps onto the first lane.
  2. Every lane she crosses raises your multiplier. Press GO to step into the next one.
  3. Any manhole might burst into flame. If it does, the hen is roasted and the bet is lost.
  4. Press CASH OUT at any time to take your bet × the current multiplier.
  5. Cross every lane to reach the Golden Egg and win the top multiplier.

Provably fair

The whole road is decided when you place the bet, from a secret server seed. Its SHA-256 hash is shown before your first step, and the seed is revealed when the road ends. Lane n is on fire when HMAC-SHA256(seed, "lane:n") (first 13 hex digits ÷ 16¹³) is below fire ÷ (26 − n), where fire is 1, 3, 5 or 10 by difficulty. Check your last road below.
