How to play

  1. Choose your bet and a difficulty, then press Play. The hen steps onto the first lane.
  2. Every lane she crosses raises your multiplier. Press GO to hop into the next one.
  3. On any lane a car might run her over — then the bet is lost.
  4. Each lane she survives gets a barrier, and the manhole she crossed turns into a gold coin.
  5. Press Cash out at any time to take your bet × the current multiplier.
  6. Cross every lane to reach the Golden Egg and win the top multiplier.

Provably fair

The whole road is decided when you place the bet, from a secret server seed. Its SHA-256 hash is shown before your first step, and the seed is revealed when the road ends. A car hits lane n when HMAC-SHA256(seed, "lane:n") (first 13 hex digits ÷ 16¹³) is below 1 − M(n−1) ÷ M(n), where M is the lane's multiplier and M(0) is the RTP. Check your last road below.
